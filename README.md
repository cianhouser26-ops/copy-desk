# Copy Desk

A one-page website that follows 34 reliable Polymarket traders. It shows their trades as they happen and alerts you when one of them buys.

Open it at https://cianhouser26-ops.github.io/copy-desk/

- Trades come from Polymarket's public data API. The page checks every wallet once a minute while it's open.
- The wallet list was picked on 2 Nov 2026: traders who are profitable overall, have 50+ resolved bets, beat the odds they paid, and stake $20 or more on a typical bet.
- Copying trades is your own call. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
