A trader who pays 50¢ is expected to win about half the time; one who wins 85% of those bets "beats the odds" by 35 points. A trader is trusted when they have 25+ such bets, win 65% or more of them, beat the odds by 10+ points, by a margin very unlikely to be luck, made money on those bets, and put no more than 35% of their bets on 90¢+ sure things.
